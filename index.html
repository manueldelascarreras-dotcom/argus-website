Questions

What happens during the pilot?

We connect ARGUS to the tools you already use. For 30 days, Radar watches them and sends you alerts. At the end, we review what it found together and you decide whether to continue.

Will ARGUS do anything without asking me?

No. ARGUS suggests an action and waits. Nothing is sent, booked, paid or ordered until you approve it.

Which tools can it connect to?

Bookings, email, WhatsApp, payments, invoices, inventory and spreadsheets. Tell us what you use when you book a pilot and we'll confirm what can be connected.

Do I need to change how I work?

No. ARGUS works on top of the tools you already have. You keep using them as usual.
